
# Phishing Awareness Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the DecodeLabs Cyber Security Internship Program.

The objective of this project was to analyze a phishing email sample, identify phishing indicators, recognize suspicious keywords and links, and explain why the message is unsafe.

This task focused on phishing awareness, threat identification, social engineering detection, and security thinking, which are essential skills for aspiring Security Analysts and SOC Analysts.

## Objectives

* Analyze a phishing email sample
* Identify suspicious links and keywords
* Recognize phishing indicators and red flags
* Explain why phishing messages are unsafe
* Develop cybersecurity awareness and prevention strategies
* Improve understanding of social engineering attacks

## Tools Used

* Email Sample Analysis
* Browser-Based Investigation
* Microsoft Word
* PDF Documentation

## Phishing Indicators Identified

### 1. Too Good To Be True Offer

The email promised a free smartphone giveaway, a common tactic used by attackers to attract victims.

### 2. Suspicious URL

The provided website link appeared suspicious and did not belong to a trusted or verified organization.

### 3. Urgency

The email created pressure by stating that the offer would expire within a limited time period.

### 4. Request for Personal Information

Users were encouraged to provide personal details in order to claim the reward.

### 5. Unknown Sender

The sender's identity could not be verified, increasing the likelihood of a phishing attempt.

## Suspicious Keywords Identified

The following keywords and phrases were identified as phishing indicators:

* Congratulations
* Free Smartphone
* Claim Your Prize
* Limited Time Offer
* Expires in 12 Hours
* Provide Personal Details

These phrases are commonly used in phishing campaigns to manipulate users into taking immediate action.

## Why the Message is Unsafe

The message is unsafe because it uses a fake reward to lure users into revealing personal information. Such phishing attacks often lead to identity theft, financial fraud, credential theft, and unauthorized access to accounts.

The email combines social engineering techniques with deceptive messaging to exploit user trust.

## Safety Recommendations

* Verify promotions through official company websites.
* Avoid clicking suspicious links.
* Never share personal information through email links.
* Check URLs carefully before entering information.
* Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
* Report suspicious emails immediately.
* Verify sender authenticity before responding.
